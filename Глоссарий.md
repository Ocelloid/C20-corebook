## Игровые механики

### Actions

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 243): Actions are anything a character does that might produce an interesting outcome to the direction of the story
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 243): One action usually takes one turn to complete

Перевод: Действия

### Simple Action

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 243): If only one success is required to accomplish an action, the action is called a simple action

Перевод: Простое действие

### Extended Actions

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 243): Actions requiring more successes or longer periods of time to complete are called extended actions
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 246): Completing some tasks can take longer than a single turn. When you need multiple successes to score even a marginal success, you are undertaking an extended action

Перевод: Продленные действия

### Reflexive Actions

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 243): When characters react instinctively, they take a reflexive action. Characters perform reflexive actions automatically, and may take a normal action on a turn without penalty

Перевод: Рефлексивные действия

### Resisted Actions

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 247): As soon as multiple characters become involved in a contest, more than a single difficulty is required
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 247): In such a case, the player makes a resisted roll — each participant rolls dice against a difficulty, and the person with the most successes wins

Перевод: Противостоящие действия

### Multiple Actions

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): Occasionally, a character needs to perform more than one action in a turn
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): In such situations, actions can be attempted normally, though all actions become more difficult as the character's attention is split among them

Перевод: Множественные действия

### Difficulties

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): When a character is about to make an action and a roll is required to determine success, the Storyteller will assign a difficulty number to the action
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): A difficulty is always a number between 2 and 10

Перевод: Сложности

### Successes

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): Each time you score that number or higher on one of your dice, you've gained a success
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): Only one success is required to perform most actions successfully, but it's a marginal success

Перевод: Успехи

### Failure

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 244): Scoring no successes on a roll results in a failed action
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 245): Failure should not represent a sudden stop to a story, but instead should present an array of possibilities to the player who failed

Перевод: Провал

### Botches

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 245): Sometimes it's just not a changeling's day. When the dice show a 1, the result is severe enough to cancel out a success
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 245): If none of the dice show as successes and one or more dice shows 1, the roll is a botch

Перевод: Ботч

### Automatic Success

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 245): Rolling dice isn't always necessary, especially when a character is so adept at a particular task that involving a chance of failure would break the game's immersion
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 245): If the number of dice in a dice pool is equal to or greater than the difficulty number, the character automatically succeeds with no dice rolled

Перевод: Автоматический успех

### Teamwork

Контекст:

- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 247): Changeling is all about teamwork, the bonds of friendship, alliance, and fealty
- Раздел `09_chapter_five_rules` (стр. 247): changelings can collect successes by working together

Перевод: Командная работа
