Near Dreaming
The Near Dreaming takes its name from its proximity to 
the Autumn Realm, creating distorted images of major cities, 
or providing a homestead or freehold with a counterpart in 
the Dreaming. In fact, since the Resurgence, it’s become quite 
common to enter the Near Dreaming by using the front door 
to a freehold in the mundane world and exit in the Dreaming 
through the back door. Not all freeholds are connected to the 
Dreaming this way, but they always have access to a trod that 
can be opened following the correct procedure.
Once inside the Dreaming, the Silver Path shines vividly 
for its travelers, and only fades once the wayfarer is closing in 
on the edges of the Far Dreaming. Time in the Near Dreaming 
flows at approximately the same rate as in the normal world, 
but due to the lack of Banality, it’s easier to use traveling Arts 
and Unleashing.
