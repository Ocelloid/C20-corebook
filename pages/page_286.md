Notes:
Difficulty: The normal difficulty at which the weapons is used.
Damage: The number of damage dice rolled when employing this weapon.
Type: B = Bashing L = Lethal A = Aggravated
Conceal: P = Pocket J = Jacket T= Trenchcoat N= N/A
+: This represents any large sword that can be used with one or two 
hands, including both broadswords and katanas.
(B): Breakable: Shatters after three uses.
(CI): Cold Iron: This weapon can be made of cold iron.
(D): Defensive: User gains +1 dice to block or disarm maneuvers.
(E): Entangling: May be used to entangle an enemy’s limb at +1 difficulty.
(P): Piercing: This weapon ignores up to two points of armor
(R): Risky: On a botch user damages self.
(t): Weapon gains +1 damage if used two handed.
(T): Two-handed weapon, +2 difficulty if used one handed.
(H): Heavy two-handed weapon, this weapon requires a minimum 
Strength of 3 and cannot be used one handed.
